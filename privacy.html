Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Make Their Day Bouquets is a small flower business in Santa Cruz County, California. This page explains what this website does and doesn't do with your information.

What we collect

This site doesn't sell anything online, take payments, or ask you to create an account. If you use the Order Flowers form, we collect what you type into it: your name, email address, what you'd like to order, your budget, the date you need your flowers, and any details you share. We use this only to reply to you and to prepare your order.

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and how they use the site. It collects anonymous usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location (city or region), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. It uses cookies to do this. We can't identify you from this data.

If you contact us

The Contact button gives you our email address. If you email us or message us on Instagram, we receive whatever you choose to send, such as your name, email address and the details of your order. We use it only to reply to you and to fill your order. We don't sell or share it.

Third-party services

  • Formspree to deliver your order request to our email inbox. Your form entries pass through this service on the way to us.
  • Google Analytics for site statistics. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.
  • Google Fonts to display the site's typefaces. Your browser requests the fonts from Google, which means Google receives your IP address.
  • Instagram if you follow the link to our profile. Instagram's own privacy policy applies there.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when pages are loaded.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works the same either way.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
